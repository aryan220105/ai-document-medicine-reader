# Common Bill Terminology

Electricity, water, and similar bills often include:

- Account or consumer number
- Bill amount or total amount payable
- Due date
- Billing period
- Units consumed
- Late-payment charges
- Customer name and address

## Simple explanation pattern

- The amount to pay is the printed bill amount or total payable.
- The due date is the printed due date or pay-by date.
- The account or consumer number identifies the connection.

If a late fee is mentioned, explain that paying after the due date may add charges according to the bill. Do not invent the fee amount.

## Common labels

- Bill Amount
- Amount Payable
- Total Due
- Due Date
- Pay By
- Account Number
- Consumer Number
- Units
- kWh
