# Insurance Document Terminology

Insurance letters and policy pages may include:

- Policy number
- Premium amount
- Due date or renewal date
- Coverage period
- Claim number
- Insurer name
- Nominee or insured person name, if printed

## How to explain

Use only printed values. A premium is the amount shown as payable to keep cover active, if that is what the document says.

Do not tell the user that a claim will be approved.
Do not invent coverage limits.

## Common labels

- Policy No
- Policy Number
- Premium
- Sum Insured
- Claim
- Renewal
- Exclusions may be listed; summarise only what is readable
