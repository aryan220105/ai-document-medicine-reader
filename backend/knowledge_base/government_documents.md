# Government Document Terminology

Government notices, ration-card forms, and official letters often contain:

- Reference or file numbers
- Dates of issue
- Deadlines or hearing dates
- Office names and departments
- Instructions to appear, submit documents, or pay a fee
- Identity or application numbers

## How to help a reader

Explain, in simple language:

1. What the paper appears to be
2. Any action requested
3. Any date or deadline printed
4. Any amount printed
5. Any reference number printed

Only use values found in the uploaded document. If a deadline is not printed, say so.

## Common words

- Notice
- Circular
- Order
- Application
- Acknowledgement
- Department
- Ward
- Taluk
- Panchayat
- Municipality
- Ration
- Aadhaar-related form language may appear; do not invent ID numbers
