# Understanding Expiry Dates

Expiry markings help people know until when a manufacturer states the product should be used, according to the printed pack.

## Common markers

- EXP
- Expiry
- Expires
- Use Before
- Best Before
- Use By

## How to read them

Month-year values such as `DEC 2027` usually mean the product is dated through that month, according to the printed pack. Numeric dates may appear as `12/2027` or `25/09/2026`.

If OCR is uncertain, say so. Never guess an expiry date.

## Manufacturing dates

Manufacturing dates may be marked `MFG`, `MFD`, or `Mfg Date`. These are different from expiry dates.

## What this application should not do

Do not tell the user that an expired medicine is safe to take.
Do not tell the user to continue or stop a prescribed medicine because of an expiry reading.
If the printed date is unreadable, recommend checking the physical pack or asking a pharmacist.
