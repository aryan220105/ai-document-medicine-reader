You are given the printed title of a blank form, the authority that appears to issue it,
and a short extract of its text. Search the web for background on this form and report
what you find.

WHAT THIS IS FOR

The application already reads the form's own pages. What it cannot get from the paper is
background that the form assumes its reader knows: what an initialism stands for in this
authority's usage, what the form is called in full, what procedure it belongs to. That
background is used only to describe what each blank asks for. It is never used to decide
what a user should write, and never presented as an instruction from the form.

RULES

1. Search for the form by its title and issuing authority. Prefer the authority's own
   site and official publications over commentary, coaching sites, and news.
2. Return only what you can support from what you found. If the search tells you nothing
   useful about this form, say so by returning empty fields and a low confidence. An
   empty answer is a good answer; a plausible invention is not.
3. "glossary" is the valuable part: initialisms and abbreviations this form uses, with
   what they stand for in this authority's usage. Do not include an expansion you are
   guessing at.
4. Never return eligibility rules, document checklists, fees, deadlines, penalties, or
   procedural steps. Those must come from the form itself, which the application already
   reads. Returning them risks telling somebody an official rule that does not apply to
   their case.
5. Never return anything about a named individual.
6. "sources" should list the pages you actually relied on, by URL or by publisher name.
7. Keep "summary" to two sentences on what the form is and what it belongs to.
8. Return valid JSON only, with no commentary before or after it.

OUTPUT SCHEMA

{
  "official_name": "the form's full official name, or empty",
  "authority": "the issuing body, or empty",
  "summary": "two sentences, or empty",
  "glossary": {"ABBR": "what it stands for in this authority's usage"},
  "sources": ["url or publisher"],
  "confidence": 0.0
}
